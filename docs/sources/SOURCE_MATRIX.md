# P0.5 source matrix

**As of:** 2026-10-07 (Europe/Madrid). Eight independent providers; three catalog candidates, two market candidates, and three Spanish retailers. A derived Cardmarket price inside TCGdex or the legacy API is **not** an independent Cardmarket feed. `VERIFIED` means only the specific bounded response observed in a fiche, never continuous operation, completeness, or permission for every use. All official references, method, unknowns, and probe counts are in the linked dated fiches.

**Evidence classes:** the linked fiches mark documented provider claims, bounded observations, and inferences separately. Only TCGdex catalog access was directly probed (5/5). Other providers were `DOCUMENTED_ONLY` as an evidence level even when their decision classification is `CONDITIONAL` or `REJECTED`. Browser reading of public terms and product pages is not counted as an API/access probe. No retained payload, token, cookie, or personal record was added.

**Coverage result:** no source in this matrix is both verified for a Spanish/EU retail or market production use and licensed for CMT's planned durable multi-source history. No evaluated source establishes an accessible, licensed feed of individual EUR completed sales; Cardmarket's documented EUR sold-price aggregates have different semantics and unavailable rights for CMT's planned use.
