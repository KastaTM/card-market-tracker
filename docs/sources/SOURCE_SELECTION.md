## Probe decision and escalation

Only TCGdex had clearly documented unauthenticated GET access suitable for a small technical probe; its five results are in the [fiche](TCGDEX.md). The three retailer fiches record why no direct monitor-style GET was made. eBay and Cardmarket lacked authorized production credentials and/or compatible terms. Awin's official product-feed path is promising for affiliate price-comparison use, but the particular Pokémon retailers, access, license, and cost have not been assessed; joining a network or accepting terms is outside this P0.5 authorization.

**AC-F05 is `FAIL`:** catalog probe exists, but no permitted retail/discovery probe exists. The Orchestrator is asked to choose whether to seek written access from GAME/Fnac/El Corte Inglés, assess an authorized affiliate/product feed as a separately scoped candidate, or revise the release sequence for P6a/P4. It must not be recorded as a passing gate by treating manual page reading or a hypothetical API response as a probe.

Market reference and historical use are a second decision: seek a rights-compatible EUR feed and define whether listings, sold transactions or provider aggregates meet the product's intended signal. EUR denomination and an `EBAY_ES` header alone do not prove Spanish supply, shipping or representativeness. The [preliminary ingestion requirements](../data-contracts/SOURCE_INGESTION_V0.md) preserve these distinctions without implementing future phases.
