## Probe decision and escalation

Only TCGdex had clearly documented unauthenticated GET access suitable for a small technical probe; its five results are in the [fiche](TCGDEX.md). The three retailer fiches record why no direct monitor-style GET was made. eBay and Cardmarket lacked authorized production credentials and/or compatible terms. Awin's official product-feed path is promising for affiliate price-comparison use, but the particular Pokémon retailers, access, license, and cost have not been assessed; joining a network or accepting terms is outside this P0.5 authorization.

**AC-F05 proposed `PASS` after RW-01:** four catalog-detail GETs and one ES set-enumeration GET supply two functional routes from the same provider. [The TCGdex assessment](TCGDEX.md#rw-01--discovery-de-sets-evaluación-de-la-evidencia-existente) explains the nonempty list, observed field structure and official semantics, plus the unexecuted comparison and missing payload/IDs. QA and REVIEWER agreed that the exact feasibility criterion does not require distinct providers or an implemented temporal detector. No additional TCGdex request was made. RW-02's Scrydex GET was conditional on RW-01 being insufficient and was therefore not executed; Scrydex remains `CONDITIONAL`, zero API requests.

**Retail decision still required:** seek written access from GAME/Fnac/El Corte Inglés, assess an authorized affiliate/product feed under a separately scoped review, or decide the release sequence for P6a and the retail part of P4. AC-F05's set-discovery evidence does not establish any retailer, sealed offer, stock or preorder capability. Manual retail page reading remains documentary evidence, not an automated probe.

Market reference and historical use are a second decision: seek a rights-compatible EUR feed and define whether listings, sold transactions or provider aggregates meet the product's intended signal. EUR denomination and an `EBAY_ES` header alone do not prove Spanish supply, shipping or representativeness. The [preliminary ingestion requirements](../data-contracts/SOURCE_INGESTION_V0.md) preserve these distinctions without implementing future phases.
