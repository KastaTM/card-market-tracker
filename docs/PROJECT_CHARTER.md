# Project charter

CMT is a personal platform for reliable collectibles market observations and opportunity discovery. The first market is Pokémon TCG, Spain/Europe, EUR; the first deployment target is a Raspberry Pi ARM64 under Docker. The authoritative product vision is in [context.md](../context.md).

Discovery of previously unknown products and a watchlist of known products are both first-class future capabilities. External listings are observations, not completed sales or established market value. A suspicious price must not automatically become an opportunity; an unknown product must not be silently discarded.

P0 establishes reproducible engineering infrastructure only. It does not deliver catalog, retail adapters, historical market data, alerts, or business models. Later phases should preserve provider-independent domain identity, historical observations, explainable signals, and source restrictions without prematurely installing unused frameworks.

Success is determined by the [P0 contract](../P0_FOUNDATION_CONTRACT.md), independent review, and Orchestrator acceptance. [Quality gates](QUALITY_GATES.md) explain the evidence required.
